03
Knowledge Graph & Local RAG

KG-RAG for ISRO Domain Question Answering

Built a domain-specific Knowledge Graph-Augmented RAG system for ISRO documentation. The pipeline crawls and chunks source documents, extracts domain entities and relations with spaCy, constructs a NetworkX knowledge graph, performs FAISS dense retrieval, and combines graph and passage context before generating answers with a locally quantized Mistral-7B model through Ollama.

Knowledge Graph RAG FAISS spaCy NetworkX Mistral-7B Ollama Streamlit ↗

Air Quality Prediction

Developed an interpretable air-quality prediction pipeline combining Ant Colony Optimization for feature selection with machine-learning models for AQI forecasting. The optimization component evaluates feature subsets, tracks convergence and pheromone evolution, separates policy-controllable from uncontrollable variables, and produces reproducible feature-selection outputs for downstream prediction.
